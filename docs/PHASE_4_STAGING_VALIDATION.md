## 3. Detailed Workflow Evidence

### 3.1 Authentication & Token Lifecycle
- Verified that tokens generated by [src/lib/jwt-secret.ts](file:///d:/yogesh/BothraCMS-main/BothraCMS-main/src/lib/jwt-secret.ts) conform strictly to RFC 7519 HMAC-SHA256 standards.
- Expired tokens generated with timestamps in the past were evaluated by the middleware engine and rejected with `ERR_JWT_EXPIRED`.

### 3.2 Role Matrix & Least Privilege
- Tested all 8 operational roles:
  - `SUPER_ADMIN` & `ORG_ADMIN`: Full administrative capabilities within the tenant.
  - `PROJECT_MANAGER`: Access scoped to assigned project.
  - `SITE_SUPERVISOR`: Prohibited from issuing payments or modifying bills.
  - `ACCOUNTANT`: Prohibited from creating store goods receipts or stock issues.
  - `STOREKEEPER`: Prohibited from creating vendor bills or disbursing payments.
  - `HR_VIEWER`: Prohibited from modifying material stock or finance records.
  - `AUDITOR`: Strictly read-only for compliance; all mutating operations blocked.

### 3.3 Multi-Tenant Isolation & Anti-IDOR
- Created `Staging Org Alpha` and `Staging Org Beta`.
- Attempted to query `Staging Horizon Towers` (belonging to Org Alpha) using an Org Beta query context.
- Result: The query returned `null`. Cross-tenant record references are completely invisible and unmodifiable.

### 3.4 Day Book & Timezone Tolerance
- Incurred financial entries with canonical noon UTC timestamps (`T12:00:00.000Z`).
- Queried via [src/app/api/day-book/route.ts](file:///d:/yogesh/BothraCMS-main/BothraCMS-main/src/app/api/day-book/route.ts) with `matchesDate` helper.
- Result: Records correctly matched both the UTC calendar day and Indian Standard Time (+05:30) calendar day, completely eliminating the date shift bug.
